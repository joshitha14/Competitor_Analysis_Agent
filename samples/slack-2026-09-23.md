## Microsoft Teams
*Why a competitor:* Direct competitor offering integrated chat, video, and collaboration tools within the Microsoft 365 ecosystem

**Pricing** — Microsoft Teams offers multiple pricing tiers including a free version, Business Basic at $6.00 per user per month (billed annually), Business Standard at $12.50 per user per month (billed annually), Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot at $23.50 per user per month (billed yearly), and Teams Enterprise at $8.55 per user per month (billed yearly). Additional add-ons such as Teams Phone, Teams Premium, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and calling plans can increase the final cost.

**Core features**
- Chat and group chat
- Video meetings and calls
- File sharing
- Integration with Microsoft 365 apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook)
- AI-powered chat and collaboration (Copilot in Teams)
- Teams Rooms and Teams Phone
- Security and access controls
- AI-generated notes, tasks, and actions from Teams Facilitator agent
- Unified device management
- Communicator app for targeted announcements with acknowledgment tracking
- Malicious URL detection in Gov clouds
- Work IQ controls and Tasks view in Copilot
- Awards and certifications as profile badges

**Positioning** — Microsoft Teams is positioned as a comprehensive collaboration platform that brings together chat, meetings, calling, file sharing, and integration with Microsoft 365 and third-party apps. It targets businesses of all sizes, from small teams to large enterprises, with scalable plans and advanced AI-driven features for productivity, security, and communication.

**Recent news**
- The Communicator app entered Public Preview in mid-August 2026, enabling targeted announcements with acknowledgment tracking.
- Teams Live Chat was retired, with the widget removal deadline set for 5 October 2026.
- Teams web moved to teams.cloud.microsoft by 30 September 2026.
- Teams Phone Agent remains in Frontier preview with AudioCodes as the only listed provider.
- Copilot in Teams received a simplified chat-centered design, improved navigation, and new Tasks view in full-screen mode.
- Microsoft Teams now detects and warns users about malicious URLs in Gov clouds.
- After recorded calls, Copilot in the Queues app helps users catch up on key details and resolve issues faster.

## Google Chat
*Why a competitor:* Built-in team chat platform within Google Workspace, providing a direct alternative for teams using Google's suite of tools

**Pricing** — Plans start at $3.5/month (9 plans available). Paid accounts for organizations start at $6 per person per month. Free users can access basic Google Chat features without a paid Workspace account.

**Core features**
- Instant messaging (1:1 and group)
- Project spaces and team collaboration
- File sharing integrated with Google Drive
- One-click Meet scheduling within Gmail and Google Workspace
- Real-time chat with typing indicators
- Advanced search and filtering capabilities for messages, files, and keywords
- Integration with Gmail, Google Drive, and other Google Workspace apps
- AI-powered features via Gemini (e.g., conversation summaries, real-time translation, action item extraction, brainstorming)
- Third-party integrations (e.g., Confluence, 1Password SaaS Manager)
- Announcement spaces and permission management via Chat API
- Support for AI agents via Google Chat Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools

**Positioning** — Designed for teams and businesses of any size, especially those already using Google Workspace. It simplifies internal communication, reduces email overload, and supports remote and cross-location collaboration. Strong adoption among small businesses (63%), education (8%), and enterprise companies (20%).

**Recent news**
- September 2026: Google Chat now includes AI Overviews in Gmail search and enhanced third-party integrations via Gemini.
- September 2026: Launch of new 1Password App for Google Chat to streamline IT and HR processes.
- September 2026: Confluence integration allows teams to bring project context directly into Chat.
- September 2026: Google Chat API now supports announcement spaces and permission settings.
- September 2026: Google Chat Model Context Protocol (MCP) server adds tools for marking messages as read/unread and listing memberships.
- December 2023: Redesign of Google Chat with updated sidebar and logo.
- May 2022: Google Chat began displaying phishing and malware warnings from personal accounts.

## Flock
*Why a competitor:* A feature-rich, affordable team communication tool with a free plan that directly competes with Slack's pricing and functionality

**Pricing** — Flock offers a free tier with unlimited messages and 10,000 searchable messages. The Pro plan is priced at $4.50/user/month (or $54/year) for teams, with yearly billing available. Enterprise pricing is custom and not publicly listed. Special pricing is available for non-profit organizations.

**Core features**
- Real-time team messaging with channels and direct chats
- Video and audio calls
- Screen sharing
- File sharing with searchable chat history
- Task management and shared to-do lists
- Third-party integrations (e.g., Google Drive)
- API-based custom alerts
- Audit assistance and searchable audit logs for law enforcement use

**Positioning** — Flock is positioned as a lightweight, cost-effective team communication and collaboration platform, particularly suitable for small to mid-sized teams seeking a simpler alternative to platforms like Slack or Microsoft Teams. It emphasizes ease of use, fast onboarding, and affordability.

**Recent news**
- In September 2026, Flock Safety (the surveillance technology arm of Flock) faced significant backlash over misuse of its license plate recognition (LPR) technology, including allegations of police stalking ex-partners. As a result, Florida and Texas stopped using the technology, and 90 cities dropped Flock in a single month.
- Flock Safety announced it would require its Audit Assistance tool for all law enforcement customers by the end of 2026 to detect abnormal activity.
- In September 2026, Flock Safety offered generous employee buyouts amid internal and public scrutiny, reportedly due to employee demoralization over the controversy.
- Sen. Josh Hawley invited Flock Safety's CEO to testify before a Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on AI surveillance, citing concerns over data collection and retention policies.
