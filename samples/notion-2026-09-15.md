## Airtable
*Why a competitor:* Offers a similar all-in-one workspace with databases, project management, and collaboration, directly competing in the structured data and workflow automation space

**Pricing** — Airtable offers multiple pricing tiers:
- Free plan: Available for solo users or small teams; includes core database functionality, basic interface designer, and limited automations.
- Team plan: Not explicitly priced in results, but mentioned as eligible for 50% off for qualifying organizations.
- Business plan: $45 per user per month when billed annually.
- Enterprise Scale plan: Custom pricing based on organization needs and scale; includes 25,000 AI credits per paid user per month, advanced admin features, SAML SSO, admin panel, two-way sync, and 100 GB of attachments per base.
- Portal add-ons: Priced per guest; start at $120/month for 15 guests on Team plan or $150/month on Business plan (2026 pricing).

**Core features**
- Core database functionality
- Basic interface designer
- Limited automations (Free plan)
- Unlimited workspaces (Business plan)
- Advanced admin-level features and security (Business plan)
- SAML SSO, admin panel, two-way sync, 100 GB of attachments (Enterprise Scale)
- 25,000 AI credits per paid user per month (Enterprise Scale)
- Standard sync integrations, extensions, Gantt and timeline views, expanded customization (Team plan)
- 125,000 records per base, 100,000 automation runs per month (Business plan)
- 100 GB of attachment space per base (Business plan)

**Positioning** — Airtable positions itself as a flexible, scalable relational database platform for teams and organizations managing multiple departments or growing adoption across clients and internal teams. It targets small to medium-sized teams (Team plan), larger organizations (Business plan), and large enterprises requiring advanced governance and administration (Enterprise Scale). The platform emphasizes customization, data scale, collaboration, and integration capabilities.

- The acquisition was completed on September 4, 2026.

## Coda
*Why a competitor:* added by user

**Pricing** — Coda uses a 'Maker billing' model, where users only pay for 'Doc Makers' (users who create and edit documents), while Editors and Viewers are free. Pricing is per Doc Maker per month.

**Core features**
- Unlimited doc size for unshared docs (Free plan)
- Connected tables, charts, Kanban boards, and forms
- Basic automations and formulas
- Cross-doc tables and advanced automations (Pro plan)
- Role-based permissions (Doc Maker, Editor, Viewer)
- Advanced permissions, priority support, admin controls (Team plan)
- Enterprise-grade security, admin oversight, and priority support (Enterprise plan)
- Coda AI bundled and included for Doc Makers (unlimited access under fair use policy)

**Positioning** — Coda is positioned as a flexible, scalable collaboration and work management platform suitable for teams of all sizes, from individuals and small teams to large enterprises. It emphasizes value, fairness in billing, and scalability.
