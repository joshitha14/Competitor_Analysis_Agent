## Jira
*Why a competitor:* Industry-standard issue tracking for software development teams, offering broader enterprise capabilities beyond Linear's engineering-only focus.

**Pricing** — Jira offers multiple pricing tiers:
- Free plan: Available for up to 10 users, includes basic views, dashboards, unlimited projects, tasks, and forms.
- Standard plan: $7/user/month (annual billing) or $7.91/user/month (monthly billing), includes AI-powered features, collaborative teamwork, customizable approval processes, and unlimited storage.
- Premium plan: $12/user/month (annual) or $14.54/user/month (monthly) for up to 300 users, includes advanced planning for cross-functional work, Atlassian Intelligence, global automation, unlimited storage, and enhanced analytics.
- Enterprise plan: Custom pricing negotiated directly with Atlassian sales, based on user count, contract length, and existing Atlassian product footprint. Includes multiple instances, centralized billing, unlimited automation, advanced insights with Atlassian Analytics, SAML-based SSO (additional $2–4/user/month), BYOK encryption, Product Requests, and Atlassian Access.
- Data Center (self-hosted): Starts at $51,000 per year, for enterprises with teams of 500 or more, includes SAML 2.0, AWS optimization, disaster recovery, and recovery options.

**Core features**
- AI-powered features (Atlassian Intelligence)
- Advanced planning for cross-functional work
- Global automation to standardize best practices
- Unlimited storage
- Collaborative teamwork and customizable approval processes
- Integration with Confluence, Loom, Rovo, and AI coding agents
- Jira Planner (Early Access)
- Formula Fields (Generally Available)
- Proactive AI and individual capacity planning (Summer 2026 release)
- Jira Cloud for Microsoft Teams app with natural language understanding
- Native integration with GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and Claude in Jira Automation
- Teamwork Graph for search, agents, and analytics (on paid plans)
- SAML 2.0, disaster recovery, and AWS optimization (Data Center)
- BYOK encryption and Product Requests (Enterprise)

**Positioning** — Jira is positioned as a scalable, AI-native project management and software development platform designed for teams of all sizes, with a focus on agile workflows, automation, and integration across the software development lifecycle. It is marketed as a system of record for engineering teams, enabling collaboration between humans and AI agents. The platform emphasizes enterprise-grade security, scalability, and governance, particularly in its Premium and Enterprise tiers.

**Recent news**
- July 15, 2026: Atlassian announced new agentic engineering tools in Jira, including Code Context (open beta), Agent loops, and AI Review (private early access), to support governed agentic workflows in the AI-native SDLC.
- Summer 2026: Jira's Summer Release includes rebuilt core views, proactive AI, individual capacity planning, formula fields, and enhancements to the project timeline and Jira Planner.
- Jira Cloud for Microsoft Teams app now supports natural language commands to create, update, and assign work without leaving the chat.

## monday dev
*Why a competitor:* Offers customizable workflows and cross-functional collaboration features, making it a strong alternative for teams needing more than just engineering-focused issue tracking.

**Pricing** — Starting from $9 per user per month. Plans are structured in tiers: Basic, Standard, Pro, and Enterprise. The Basic plan is ideal for small teams and startups, offering unlimited free viewers, unlimited items, Gantt charts, Kanban boards, 5GB of storage, and dedicated development templates. Pricing increases with team size, with a minimum of 3 seats and plans ascending in multiples of 5. For teams over 40 users, a custom quote is available. Monthly billing is available, though the yearly plan offers a discount. The final price is visible on the purchase page before payment.

**Core features**
- Sprint planning
- Roadmap tracking
- Native Git integrations
- Unlimited boards
- Kanban boards
- Gantt charts
- Unlimited items
- Dedicated development templates
- 5GB of storage (on Basic plan)
- Unlimited free viewers

**Positioning** — Built for software development teams managing sprints, roadmaps, and product feedback. Designed to support agile product and software development workflows.
